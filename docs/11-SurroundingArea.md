## The Region at a Glance

Eagle Bluff sits on **Herron Island** in southern Puget Sound, reached from the **Key Peninsula** mainland near **Lakebay** and **Purdy**. The nearest major airport is **SeaTac**; Tacoma and Gig Harbor are the closest cities for services and day trips.

| Place | Role |
|-------|------|
| **Lakebay / Purdy** | Mainland ferry dock area (201 Isted Rd NW) |
| **Key Peninsula (SR-302)** | Last mainland stop for groceries, fuel, pharmacy |
| **Gig Harbor** | Dining, shopping, marina town (~day trip) |
| **Tacoma** | Full services, hospital, airport alternative routes |
| **SeaTac** | Primary airport for most guests |

## Driving to the Mainland Ferry Dock

**Dock address:** 201 Isted Rd NW, Lakebay, WA

Use Google Maps, Apple Maps, or Waze for live traffic. Allow time **beyond** GPS estimates for ferry wait and crossing — see [Ferry Guide](30-FerryArrivalGuide.md).

### From SeaTac Airport

Roughly **55–65 miles**, **1 hour 15 min – 1 hour 45 min** (traffic dependent), **plus ferry time**.

1. I-5 South toward Tacoma
2. Exit 154B → WA-518 West
3. WA-16 West toward Key Peninsula
4. SR-302 across Purdy Bridge
5. Herron Rd KPN → Isted Rd NW → dock

### From Tacoma / Olympia

Generally: **WA-16** → **SR-302** → **Herron Rd KPN** → **Isted Rd NW**

### From Seattle / North

Generally: **I-5 South** → **WA-16 West** → **SR-302** → dock

## Leaving for SeaTac (Example)

From mainland dock to SeaTac: roughly **1 hr 15 min – 1 hr 45 min**, plus ferry wait.

1. Ferry to mainland (201 Isted Rd NW)
2. Isted Rd NW → Herron Rd KPN → SR-302
3. Purdy Bridge → WA-16 East → I-5 North
4. Exit 154B → WA-518 West → Sea-Tac

Build in buffer for flights — check [ferry schedule and cancellations](31-FerrySchedules.md) before checkout day.
