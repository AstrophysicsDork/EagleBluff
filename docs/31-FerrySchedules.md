## How to Read the Schedule

Each run is listed as a **pair**:

| Code | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **IS** | Departs **Herron Island** (island side) |
| **ML** | Departs **mainland** dock (201 Isted Rd NW, Lakebay) |

Example: `IS 6:15` / `ML 7:00` — island boat leaves 6:15 AM, mainland boat leaves 7:00 AM. Crossing is ~7–10 minutes.

Schedules are grid by day of week (Mon–Sun). Summer and winter grids **differ** — use the PDF for your travel dates.

## Seasonal Schedules

### Winter 2025–2026

**Effective:** October 6, 2025 through April 30, 2026  
**Source:** [winter PDF](https://www.hmchi.org/file/document/3715548852/kDdmTUUlSqBv0LCn.pdf) (updated 3/25/26)

**Typical weekday pattern** (confirm in PDF for your day):

- Early morning through mid-morning: roughly **6:15 AM – 11:00 AM** (IS/ML pairs every ~30–60 min)
- Midday gap on many days (no continuous hourly service)
- Afternoon/evening: roughly **2:30 PM – 9:00 PM** (IS/ML pairs)

**Notes from winter schedule:**

- Runs marked **\*** may be limited to **one shuttle** to allow mandatory crew breaks and USCG work limits
- **Friday / Saturday (winter):** reduced service — **no mid-day run** on standard winter grid; check PDF
- **Holiday schedules** differ (Thanksgiving, Christmas Day, New Year's Day) — see winter PDF holiday section

### Summer 2026

**Effective:** May 1 through September 13, 2026  
**Approved:** April 21, 2026  
**Source:** [summer PDF](https://www.hmchi.org/file/document/3715548852/UTc00gbK8FFUaV1K.pdf)

**Generally more frequent** than winter, with longer evening service on weekends. Day-by-day columns differ — **use the PDF** rather than assuming the same times every day.

**Notes from summer schedule:**

- Shuttles run when vehicles/passengers follow **arrival time cut-off** and **tow/oversize notification** policies
- Departure times **may vary** for shuttles, mandatory breaks, or USCG work limits
- Refer to HMCHI **Ferry Access Policy** for cut-off and oversize rules

## Cancellations & Low Tide (Critical)

The [cancellations PDF](https://www.hmchi.org/file/document/3715548852/7IYLQ4JsXqjXkL4c.pdf) is updated regularly (e.g. April 2026). **Check it on every travel day.**

### What the terms mean

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **CANCELLED** | That run will not operate |
| **NO SHUTTLE** | Tides too low to safely operate — generally when tides are **−1.5 feet or less** |
| **Add** | Extra run outside the normal schedule |

### Tide references

HMCHI recommends:

- [Tides and Currents (NOAA)](https://tidesandcurrents.noaa.gov/) — **Ballow Station #9446583**
- Tide Charts app (7th Gear)

### Holiday weekend restrictions (examples from 2026 bulletin)

On some holiday weekends, vehicles **towing anything** and single vehicles **longer than 25 feet** are **not transported after the 12:00 noon–12:30 PM run** for several days. Special holiday schedules may be published separately (Memorial Day, July 4th, Labor Day, etc.).

Always read the current cancellations PDF for your dates.

## Practical Planning Tips

1. **Download or screenshot** the seasonal schedule and cancellations PDF before arrival — cell coverage on the island is variable.
2. **Arrive before your intended departure** — shuttles depend on cut-off policies and load.
3. **Build buffer time** for flights and checkout — missed or cancelled runs happen.
4. **Low tide** is the most common reason runs disappear from the normal grid.
5. Schedule updates may also appear at [herronisland.org](https://www.herronisland.org) per HMCHI bulletin.
