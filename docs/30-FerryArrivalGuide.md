## Important: This Is Not a Washington State Ferry

The Herron Island ferry is **not** operated by Washington State Ferries (WSDOT). Do not use the WSF website or app for this crossing.

Herron Island is a **private island**. The ferry is operated by the [Homeowners' Association of Herron Island (HMCHI)](https://www.hmchi.org/).

**Official ferry page:** [hmchi.org/p/FERRY](https://www.hmchi.org/p/FERRY)

---
