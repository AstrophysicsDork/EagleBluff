## Description (as published)

Perched on a **70-foot bluff** above Puget Sound, this Herron Island vacation rental is built around the view. Walls of windows and 2 spacious decks create a front-row seat to passing boats, glowing sunsets, and the occasional orca sighting offshore. Relax around the fire pit, take a moderate walk to South Beach Park, or explore quiet island trails where bald eagles and deer are common sights.
