
Congratulations. The first two bookings are a big milestone.

For your **first guests ever**, I would not ask for a 5-star review directly.

Instead, communicate:

1. You're excited to host them.
2. You're available if anything isn't right.
3. You genuinely want feedback.
4. You'll work hard to make the stay great.

That tends to generate better reviews than asking for stars.

I'd create a reusable template like this:

Hi Mark,

We're looking forward to hosting you at Eagle Bluff Retreat!

Your arrival is coming up soon, and we wanted to reach out to welcome you. Herron Island is a special place, and we hope you'll enjoy the quiet, the views, the wildlife, and the chance to slow down for a few days.

If you have any questions before arrival, please don't hesitate to ask. During your stay, if anything isn't quite right or if there's anything we can do to make your visit better, please let us know. We'll do our best to help.

Safe travels, and we look forward to welcoming you soon!

Jeff
Eagle Bluff Retreat

For a **day-of-arrival message**, I'd use:

Welcome to Eagle Bluff Retreat!

We hope you have a wonderful stay on Herron Island. If you need anything during your visit, please send a message and we'll do our best to help.

A few local tips:
• Ferry schedules can change, so it's worth planning return trips in advance.
• The best wildlife viewing is often early morning and around sunset.
• If you're lucky, you'll spot eagles soaring over the bluff.

Most importantly, relax and enjoy the island.

Jeff

Then, after checkout, I'd send something like:

Hi Mark,

Thank you for staying at Eagle Bluff Retreat. We hope you had a great visit and enjoyed your time on Herron Island.

As a new host, we're continually improving the guest experience. If there was anything we could have done better, we'd genuinely appreciate hearing about it.

Thank you again for choosing Eagle Bluff Retreat, and we'd love to welcome you back in the future.

Jeff

One additional thought: because these are literally your first bookings, consider adding a sentence to the pre-arrival message such as:

> "You're among our very first guests, and we're excited to share Eagle Bluff Retreat with visitors for the first time."

Many guests react positively to being part of the beginning of something new. It also subtly explains why you're being attentive without sounding desperate for reviews.

The bigger review strategy isn't the message. It's responding **fast**. For first-time hosts, a 2-minute response to questions is often worth more than any carefully crafted review request. Guests remember responsiveness.
