# Executive Summary

Eagle Bluff Retreat is a short-term vacation rental property located on Herron Island, Washington.

The property is intended to operate as a professionally managed vacation rental while also potentially serving as a marketing vehicle for future property sales ("try before you buy").

The goal is not merely to rent a house.

The goal is to offer an island retreat experience centered around nature, quiet, scenery, wildlife, and disconnection from urban life.
